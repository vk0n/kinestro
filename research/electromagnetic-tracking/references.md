# Primary reference register

Sources checked on 2026-11-15. A publication's result, a manufacturer's specification, an analytical deduction, and a Kinestro simulation are different evidence classes. None of these sources establishes Kinestro hardware performance. No external source, code, design, dataset, or figure is copied or relicensed here.

## Physical models and observability

| ID | Primary source and date | Relevance and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Raab, Blood, Steiner and Jones, *Magnetic Position and Orientation Tracking System*, IEEE TAES, September 1979, pp. 709–718. [Full paper](https://ohiostate.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/45/2020/01/raabetal.pdf) | Three-axis source/sensor coupling, independent excitation states, quasi-static dipole approximation, and iterative pose tracking. This is the original paper hosted by an educational publisher, not a new Kinestro measurement. |
| R2 | Wahlström et al., *Sensor Fusion for Magneto-Inductive Navigation*, 18 September 2019. [Full author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.08356v1), [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.08356) | Signed triaxial channel model, position/orientation estimation, hemispherical ambiguity, motion during excitation, priors, and distortion detection. [Full article](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/21/8/2822), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/s21082822) | Finite transmitter/receiver geometry, mutual inductance, and field integration across extended pickup coils. Eight frequency-labelled transmitter coils recover five sensor degrees of freedom. Physical and simulated comparisons are explicitly separated. Its planar medical setup is not a wearable torso transmitter. |

## Alternative excitation and follow-up reading

- **R4 — Anderson, US5453686A, published 26 September 1995.** [Patent text](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5453686A/en). Describes pulsed-DC position/orientation measurement and timing intended to reduce eddy-current influence. A patent establishes a disclosed arrangement, not validated Kinestro accuracy, safety, or freedom to operate. Ferromagnetic distortion remains a different mechanism from transient conductive response.
- **R5 — Li, Bien and Rose, November 2014; online 22 August 2014.** *Construction of a conductive distortion reduced electromagnetic tracking system for computer assisted image-guided interventions*. [Publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25156154/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.medengphy.2014.07.018). Original work on conductive-distortion mechanisms. Only the accessible record/abstract was checked; no uninspected numerical results are used.

Missing evidence includes comparable power-versus-range curves for wearable sources, low-cost receiver noise at the proposed excitation frequencies, on-body distortion, and two-wireless-receiver timing performance. Those gaps require the [bench experiment](bench-validation-plan.md), not interpolation between unrelated product specifications.
